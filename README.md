### Hi, I'm Thomas Hammoudi

I'm a photographer and I make photography videos in French on YouTube ([thomashammoudi.com](https://www.thomashammoudi.com)).
Most of what I publish here started as a tool I needed for my own work, and I share it as it is. Apart from
premiere-rough-cut, the docs are in French for now.

**Video editing**

- [premiere-rough-cut](https://github.com/ThomasHMD/premiere-rough-cut): Claude Code skills that rough-cut talking-head
  footage straight into Adobe Premiere Pro. Retakes and silences removed, doubts flagged, captions fixed, overlays on the
  exact word.

**Fujifilm and Lightroom**

- [SpektraLR](https://github.com/ThomasHMD/SpektraLR): analog film looks in Lightroom Classic, 20 creative profiles and a
  plug-in that develops your RAW files with halation and grain.
- [fuji-xrfc-preset-generator](https://github.com/ThomasHMD/fuji-xrfc-preset-generator): Fujifilm presets for X RAW
  Studio, measured recipes converted for 16 cameras.
- [2FujiRaw](https://github.com/ThomasHMD/2FujiRaw): unlocks Fujifilm Film Simulations in Lightroom Classic for Hasselblad
  X2D II 100C files.

**Old cameras**

- [retro-ingenierie-boitiers](https://github.com/ThomasHMD/retro-ingenierie-boitiers): film looks recreated as
  straight-out-of-camera JPEGs on the Nikon D40 (patched firmware) and D750 (Picture Control over USB).

**Around the house**

- [kindle-dashboard](https://github.com/ThomasHMD/kindle-dashboard): an old Kindle 4 as an always-on e-ink screen
  (weather, calendar, live public transport, drawing of the day).
